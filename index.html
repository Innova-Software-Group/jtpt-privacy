Common questions

I can’t sign in

Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen to get a reset code by email. If it doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder or email us.

My account says it’s awaiting approval

New accounts are approved by your Trainer. Once they’ve approved you, tap “Check status” and the app will open.

How do I delete my account?

Email jt-the-pt@outlook.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my JTPT account”. See the Privacy Policy for details.
